
Experiment 3 symbolic fitting was completed for the mildly pruned KAN-ODE model.

The symbolic fitting produced eight explicit latent-dynamics equations:
dz_1/dt through dz_8/dt.

The extracted expressions were highly complex:
- Operation counts ranged from 436 to 525.
- Expression lengths ranged from 5,472 to 6,571 characters.

Because the equations are long and nested, the symbolic result is useful as evidence that KAN symbolic extraction is possible, but it is not compact enough for direct clinical interpretation.
